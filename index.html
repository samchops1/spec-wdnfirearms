Hands on

What we teach in class

You will learn how to draw, holster, load, unload, reload, tactical and emergency reloads, maintenance and storage. Handle a pistol safely.

If you are a new shooter, don’t worry — we will get you there. Have fun.

Safety gear you will need is provided (hearing and eye). Pistols are provided if you don’t have one — no rental fee. You will have to purchase any ammo you use from WDN Firearms Training LLC.

CHP class day

Classroom, then live fire.

This training is in three sections. The online section is a course preview — upon receipt of deposit we email documents to review prior to the actual classroom, including Colorado use of force laws, concealed carry laws and regulations, situational awareness, mindset, methods of carry, and a comprehensive review of handgun safety and concealed carry law.

Safety / Basic Pistol / CHP: 0900–1600. Live fire range: 1630–1830 at Silver Bullet Range, 5901 W 38th Ave, Wheat Ridge, CO 80212. Range fee is $15.00.
